LiXee-Assist is the companion app of the LiXee-Box, the Zigbee gateway that reads the French Linky electricity meter through the ZLinky module. Set up the box, follow your consumption and control your home, from a phone, a tablet, a wall panel or your TV.

<b>Set up and reach your boxes</b>
• Box setup over Bluetooth and connection to your WiFi, step by step.
• Add a box by hand, or with a QR code scanned by the phone's camera app.
• Open the box's interface at home or remotely through its secure tunnel: the app picks the right path on its own.

<b>Understand your consumption</b>
• Live power and power cut risk: know before the breaker trips.
• On three-phase meters, one gauge per phase; with solar panels, live injection.
• The last 24 hours, hour by hour, by tariff (off-peak, peak, Tempo) and by sub-meter.
• Gas and water next to electricity.
• Tap a chart to open it full screen: every hour is detailed, compared with your budget and your usual hour.

<b>Save money</b>
• The electricity weather: the current tariff, its price, today's and tomorrow's Tempo colour, and when to run the big appliances.
• Your month against the budget set in the box, day by day.
• Your base load: what keeps running when everything seems off, and what it costs over a year.
• Your subscription compared with your real power peaks.
• Base, Off-peak or Tempo: which regulated tariff option would cost you the least, computed on your own consumption.
• Load shedding explained simply, with a button to power devices back on.

<b>Control your home</b>
• Shutters (set by percentage), Zigbee plugs and sensors.
• Action groups started with one tap.
• Thermostats: setpoint and mode of each zone.
• Home screen widgets: consumption, production, balance, device, action group, thermostat.

<b>Kiosk mode</b>
A phone or tablet on the wall becomes an always-on, full-screen touch dashboard: Energy, Savings, Devices, Groups and Thermostats, one swipe apart. Ideal on a Sonoff NSPanel Pro wall panel.

<b>On Android TV</b>
The same views with the remote. Send a box from the phone to the TV with a QR code, no keyboard needed.

No extra account: the app connects straight to your LiXee-Box, at home or through its tunnel.

<b>Free build</b>
This build is fully free software: it contains neither Firebase nor Google services. It therefore receives no push notifications and has no built-in QR code reader (the phone's camera app replaces it). Past Tempo colours, used to compare tariffs, come from the free third-party service api-couleur-tempo.fr (RTE data).
